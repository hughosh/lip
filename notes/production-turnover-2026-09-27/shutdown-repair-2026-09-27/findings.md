# Bounded shutdown repair findings (2026-09-27)

The observed q01 anomaly is reproducible from the actual `shutdown.stop -> rig.close` sequence. `rig.close` stops the writer after a successful drain. Before `os.Exit`, the still-running owner can probe the now-closed writer and raise `STORE_UNHEALTHY`; submitting that anomaly then fails with `ANOMALY_SUBMISSION_FAILED`. A temporary variant that disables the new successful-close guard makes `TestRuntimeStoreAuthorizedCloseDoesNotRaiseFailure` fail at the intended assertion; `pre-fix-reproduction.log` preserves that failure.

The contained partial repair serializes the owner health probe with the final close and exempts the closed writer only after `rig.close` succeeds. A refused close leaves the guard active. Tests cover actual successful close, an in-flight probe, refusal followed by an unexpected writer exit and durable stop, and the existing unexpected-exit behavior. `package.log` passed before a temporary owner-pause experiment was removed. `race-final.log` is the final-state focused race result and passed.

**Not a complete shutdown repair.** The owner continues processing other callbacks during close and may submit telemetry/state/anomalies after the store closes. The monitor and result loop also remain active; the result loop can race the final drain/close and submit anomalies after the writer exits. An owner-only pause experiment was removed because it left the monitor running and could create `OWNER_STALLED` during a prolonged stop. Coordinated quiescence of all runtime producers before final close is still required, with a refusal path that restores operation. Existing `rig.close` also stops alerts and releases the instance lock on a store-Shutdown refusal; that behavior needs explicit ownership/retry review. Do not use these receipts as q01 requalification or live readiness.
