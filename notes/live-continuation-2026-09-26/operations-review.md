## Findings

1. **Durable recovery is implemented, but an actual recovery has not been demonstrated here.** Normal start checks disk headroom before exchange construction (`go/cmd/harness/main.go:274-287`); the run path refuses an absent or empty operational DB (`go/cmd/harness/runtime.go`, `requireExistingDB`), and `hstore.Open` inspects existing schema and reconciles its append-only anomaly journal (`go/harness/hstore/sqlite.go:77-125`, `go/harness/hstore/writer.go`). A set or unreadable latch blocks adding; `-resume` enters wind-down without clearing it (`go/cmd/harness/main.go:342-358`; `go/harness/lifecycle/latch.go`). The runbook's independent complete exchange walk, restoration of a lost ownership ledger, and verified-flat closeout are operator actions (`notes/cr1-operations-runbook.md:1-12, 73-100`), not outcomes established by unit tests. Do not infer flatness from store refusal, a stopped process, or absence of a latch.

2. **Disk protection is on the owner path.** `newOwner` enables the guard and `evaluate` calls it (`go/cmd/harness/run.go:536,2168`). It samples DB, WAL, SHM, journal and optional qualification evidence at first tick and at most every minute; unknown or below-1-GiB free space raises `DISK_HEADROOM` SEV1 and requests a durable stop (`go/cmd/harness/operations_readiness.go:18-112`). The composed test `TestOperationsDiskRuntimeLatchesAndKeepsReducerAndObservation` asserts the latch, continued reducing/portfolio reads and a non-adding heartbeat; other tests cover the reserve boundary, unknown measurement, distinct evidence volume and symlink target (`go/cmd/harness/operations_readiness_test.go`). `ArtifactBytes` measures sizes but does not itself stop on accelerating growth. The hourly trend and archive discipline are runbook/operator checks. The runtime guard depends on the owner ticking; an externally missed heartbeat remains the independent detector of a wedged or dead process.

3. **Supervision currently contradicts exit classification.** `main.go:52-65,139-152` has distinct `exitDrained=0`, `exitFailed=1`, and `exitRefused=2`, explicitly saying a structural refusal should not retry. The rendered plist has unconditional `KeepAlive=true` and `RunAtLoad=true` (`go/harness/lifecycle/launchd.go:180-195`), so every exit restarts, including refused starts and clean drains. Deploy-time checks (`go/cmd/harness/deploy.go:138-210`) catch a wrong rung and missing DB at install time, and `go/cmd/harness/deploy_test.go` checks those cases, but they cannot prevent a later historical ticker becoming ineligible. The coordinator's reported 327,862-run obsolete job is consistent with this reachable policy; the present review did not independently inspect the job. `-live` in argv is insufficient to arm a write without the per-write `live_ok` check (`go/cmd/harness/main.go:267-272`); neither fact makes a restart loop an acceptable supervisor state.

4. **Transport delivery is not human acknowledgment.** Production composition requires an ntfy sender and HTTPS dead-man endpoint before account access (`go/cmd/harness/alerts.go:29-51`; `go/harness/ping/service.go:199-240`). Durable pending anomalies retry after failure and across restart; due SEV1 sends immediately at urgent priority, with a five-minute *deduplication bucket* (`go/harness/ping/service.go:470-636`). A 2xx from ntfy marks the anomaly `delivered_ms` (`go/harness/ping/ntfy.go:83-135`; `go/harness/hstore/sqlite.go:812-840`), which records transport acceptance, not receipt by a primary, human acknowledgment, backup delivery, or incident resolution. The dead-man code checks in on hourly heartbeat and retries failed check-ins (`go/harness/ping/service.go:312-354`; `go/harness/ping/deadman.go`), but this process cannot detect its own missing heartbeat. The runbook requires an external alarm, immediate primary page, five-minute backup page if unacknowledged, and provider receipts from an end-to-end drill (`notes/cr1-operations-runbook.md:107-126`). No in-process primary/backup acknowledgment or escalation path appears in the reviewed runtime; that requirement remains **missing observed external configuration/drill evidence**, not a green result from local alert tests.
