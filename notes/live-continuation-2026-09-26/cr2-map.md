# CR-2 turnover architecture map (read-only, 2026-09-26)

`lip-1in` remains open. The current candidate is a one-market runtime; it must not be described as continuous participation across turnover. This review made no production edits, ran no tests, and used no account access.

## Existing seam and one-market assumptions

- `go/harness/lifecycle/startup.go:624-1000` (`Startup.attempt`) reads complete account positions/orders/fills, classifies ownership through `ForeignGuard`, cancels and verifies **owned** invalid orders, and computes `managedSet(effectiveSelection, held, kept)` at :1191. `Adoption.States()` makes every nonzero held market `REDUCING`. This is the correct authority boundary; a foreign order is excluded from the owned portfolio and never passed to the cancel sweep. The returned managed set is currently diagnostic only in the runtime.
- `go/cmd/harness/runtime.go:575-825` (`newRigWithLock`) constructs `tickers := []string{c.Ticker}` before adoption, passing that singleton to `lifecycle.NewStartup`, `wsx.NewGate`, `wsx.NewSupervisor`, and `core.NewRig`. `wsx.Supervisor.dial` (`go/harness/wsx/supervisor.go:213-250`) subscribes this fixed list on each reconnect; `Gate` also fixes its ticker map at construction (`go/harness/wsx/gate.go:222-240`). `serveWithShutdown` (`run.go:586-765`) adopts before starting the supervisor/poller, but cannot simply pass adoption's managed set to already-constructed collaborators. Live discovery of a newly held ticker needs an atomic, fail-closed subscription/gate/book update or a deliberately staged reconnect.
- `go/cmd/harness/run.go:85-430,528-580,866-895` stores one `owner.market`, one schedule, one program membership, one book SID and side-indexed cancel/queue state; `install` takes only `a.States()[cfg.Ticker]`. `evaluate` at :2165-2490, `decideSide`/`build`/`targetPrice`/`targetSize`/`fundedReducer` at :2506-2960, `restingOn` at :3038, and `publish` at :3870 publish/act on only `cfg.Ticker`. `noteReduce` at :1603 ignores other tickers. `scheduleLoop` at :1374 and `programMembership` in `program_membership.go` also track only that ticker. A held old ticker therefore has neither an owner reducer nor schedule/book/monitor sample.
- `run.go:1855-2070` and `risk.TradingPnL.Evaluate` (`go/harness/risk/pnl.go:234-287`) deliberately require every nonflat position and every ledger market. `mark4` at :2013 explicitly refuses any ticker other than `cfg.Ticker`; this is an honest unevaluable P&L, not a valid turnover implementation. `risk.Snapshot` supports a list of markets, but `publish` sends exactly one selected `MarketSnap`; A5 cannot certify inherited markets from this output.
- `go/cmd/harness/config.go:46-53,271-365` requires one operator ticker and has no CR-2 mode or selection policy. `go/cmd/harness/program_membership.go` merely stops/resumes that same ticker as active membership changes. The selector/ranker, rotation clock/hysteresis, and active-program candidate universe described in `notes/harness-spec.md` §10.1 are not runtime-connected.

## Funding and risk boundary

- `go/cmd/harness/funding_cap.go:43-125` (`resolveFundingCap`) reads one selected market's `MarketFunding`, then complete account positions/resting orders. It **refuses** nonselected exposure and sums all commitments into one selected-shard cap; recovery-only disables adds for inherited commitments. This refusal must remain until CR-2's shard model is actually connected. `funding_runtime.go:33-78` polls only the selected shard and subtracts **all** live exposure from that shard. A multi-market version needs a fresh, authoritative market-to-`exchange_index`/subaccount map, one immutable run cap and current cash observation per funded shard, and a placement check charged to the request's shard. Missing/stale or conflicting shard identity blocks new adds; reductions need their own available-cash check and must not spend another shard's cash.
- `go/harness/risk/capital.go:65-242` already computes aggregate deployed collateral, a per-market adding cap, reducer-first global budget, and worst-case fundability. `go/cmd/harness/run.go:2971-3034` (`exposures`) includes positions and owned live/inflight/unknown orders across tickers, but pending reservation bookkeeping uses `cfg.Ticker`; `capital_dispatch.go:14-95` likewise only matches selected-ticker pending orders and estimates other-market reducer need at settlement value. Extend identity/reservations by `(market, side, coid)` and preserve aggregate caps while enforcing shard-local cash. Do not use account cash or one market's cash as interchangeable funding.
- `go/harness/risk/position.go:869-1011` replaces positions only from a complete account-wide walk and retains the union of old and new tickers. Keep that authority. Rotation may remove a market from **selection** only after its owned exposure and all possibly-live orders are resolved; it may leave **management** only after complete truth proves flat and no owned resting/sending/unknown order remains. Foreign orders are neither our commitment to cancel nor a licence to add beside them; `foreign_orders.go` currently requests a durable global stop on live foreign orders.

## Smallest next offline implementation chunk

Introduce a pure CR-2 `ManagedPlan`/snapshot builder at the harness boundary, under an explicit disabled-by-default CR-2 configuration path. Inputs: one complete account-wide positions and resting-orders walk, ownership classification backed by the existing ledger/reservation resolver, selected candidates, authoritative market/shard identities and fresh scoped cash. Output: sorted `selected`, `managed = selected ∪ nonflat positions ∪ possibly-live owned orders`, per-market initial state (`REDUCING` for inherited inventory), shard assignment/cap evidence, and explicit foreign-order exclusions. Reject incomplete truth, unresolved ownership, shard conflict, or absent funding. Keep the existing one-market refusal unchanged. This is a data/authority chunk, **not** an order-capable turnover release; it creates a reviewable contract for later dynamic feed/owner/funding wiring without granting adds.

Offline regressions should use fake REST/ledger fixtures: selected B with held A survives restart/rotation and plans A as managed/reducing; an owned resting/UNKNOWN order on flat A keeps A managed; a foreign-only A is excluded and never enters cancel input; incomplete positions/orders or missing A shard cash fail closed; two markets on distinct shards retain separate cash while aggregate exposure still counts both; removal requires a later complete flat/no-owned-order snapshot. Include a composed scenario or mutation at the eventual runtime seam that replaces `managed` with `selected` and is caught by observing A's subscription, mark, quantity-capped reducer, schedule, monitor sample, and clean exit after restart. A pure builder test alone cannot prove that wiring.

Following chunks are required before CR-2 is claimable: feed/gate/core universe changes on rotation and reconnect; per-market owner state, schedules, reducers and order identities; per-shard cash observations plus aggregate risk/P&L/monitor publication; selection/ranking and hysteresis; composed fake-exchange restart/turnover/foreign-order tests and attended observed cleanup. Preserve H-ORD-5b/H-SEL-11 and H-CAP-1–8; no read-only or local result authorizes financial writes or unattended operation.
