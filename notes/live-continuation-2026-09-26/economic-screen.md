## Terms and fee evidence

The [official July 30 clean terms](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/regulatory/notices/Liquidity%20Incentive%20Program%20-%20July%2030%2C%202026%20Modification%20to%20July%2015%2C%202026%20Update.pdf) returned HTTP 200 at 23:50:51 UTC; its SHA-256 `2e85332ac95b2561df0c873323186dd66d2bc6df85abc0fa01128558ab9d3eed` equals the earlier saved copy. The clean terms extend the published program through January 1, 2027, subject to amendment or termination. One random-offset snapshot per second counts only while the market is open and both sides have at least Target Size. The reference is the first descending price level at which cumulative depth reaches one fifth of target; qualifying bids extend through the level reaching full target. Side scores are normalized after distance discounts. The period reward is a *pool*, multiplied by the eligible-snapshot fraction and each participant's score share; amounts below $1 are unpaid. Individual periods and membership can end sooner.

The public [series endpoint](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-series) reports `fee_type` and `fee_multiplier` for each sampled series. Ten report `quadratic`, multiplier 1; `KXCPIYOY` reports `quadratic_with_maker_fees`, multiplier 1. The [`quadratic` fee convention](https://kalshi.com/fee-schedule) means a resting maker fill has no listed maker surcharge, while immediate taker exits incur fees; `quadratic_with_maker_fees` adds a maker fee. **The live fee-schedule page and PDF both returned HTTP 429 to the direct fetch**, so this screen confirms current API fee classification, not an authoritative current numerical fee rate or actual fill debits. Recheck fee display and debits before any attended trade. Historical [fee help](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823805-fees) confirms fee types can vary by market.
