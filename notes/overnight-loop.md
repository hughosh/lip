# Supervised driver workflow

The old overnight policy is superseded by [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md),
[verification-workflow.md](verification-workflow.md), and the
[bounded-worker protocol](../loop/protocol/RULES.md). Historical run logs remain
receipts, not instructions to continue a trading repair or start an automation.

The active agent may implement ordinary authorized work directly. Use fresh,
short-lived workers for independent questions or contained edits, and record
what each returned. Check current work before editing and preserve it. Do not
require a clean tree, DNS probes, caffeinate, or a model courier cycle for local
process/document work. Long timed operational runs need their own explicit
supervision and sleep-prevention plan.

Ordinary implementation completion and release promotion are distinct. Follow
the verification workflow's risk-based checks and receipt rules. Full catalogue
outcomes belong at exact candidate milestones; no closure, model endorsement,
or local GREEN authorizes account access or orders. The unattended conductor
remains disabled.
