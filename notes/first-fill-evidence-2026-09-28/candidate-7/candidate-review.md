## What changed from candidate-6

Candidate-6's manifest lists 291 files and candidate-7's 292. They differ in exactly three, all from this bead: `go/cmd/harness/run.go` (`5af80707…` → `06e6462e…`), `go/cmd/harness/turnover_runtime.go` (`9d73c42a…` → `6a797911…`) and the new `go/cmd/harness/phantom_reducer_test.go` (`91680132…`). No parallel-session Go change is included. lip-1ug and lip-89x are not bundled.

- **lip-14o: a side holding an unverified cancel is swept again until a complete read confirms it** (`go/cmd/harness/run.go`, 170 lines added and 3 changed; `turnover_runtime.go`, 9 lines).
  - **The defect.** In the R2 stage the harness held q = −12 in REDUCING and requoted its YES reducer under §6.5 before the first 5 s orders walk had listed it. The cancel leg named the order from `o.pending` (`unlistedOn`), the DELETE answered 200, and the verifying read still listed the order inside Kalshi's ~1.5 s list lag, so the sweep was not `Absent`. The owner released the intents and, correctly under H-FAIL-3, kept the pending entry. The next complete walk omitted the cancelled order, which under H-ORD-2a retires nothing. From then on `restingOn` counted the entry, so the reducing side read as already carrying |q|; `Decide` saw our order at the touch and asked for nothing; `escalateUnresolved` skipped it as acked; and the only re-sweep `evaluateMarket` performed was for a side being turned off (`!wanted`), never for a side the state still wanted. No reducer rested for 20 min 48 s and nothing alarmed. A planned drain would have sat at `DRAIN_TIMEOUT` for ever, because `anyLiveOrder` counts `len(o.pending)`.
  - **Why this direction.** H-ORD-4 (spec lines 870-882) already says an order still unconfirmed after the retry "stays live, fillable and in the cancellation obligation, and the next tick sweeps it again", and `rest/cancel.go`'s `sweepPageBound` is written on the assumption that the caller does so. H-ORD-4c says "absence and elapsed time never count toward retirement", which rules out the bead's second direction (retire the entry once a later walk omits it). So the owner was the component breaking an existing contract, and the fix restores it rather than adding a new semantics. The bead's third direction (escalate an acked entry after N walks) is an alarm, not a fix, and is not bundled.
  - **`owner.cancelUnverified [2]bool`**, per side, next to `cancelConfirmed`, with `cancelUnverifiedAt` and `resweptAt` for its cadence. Set in `applyWriteResult`'s cancel branch when a result that NAMED at least one order is not `Absent` (a complete read found one of them still resting, or no complete read at all). A sweep that named nothing — the verifying read `build` issues for an UNKNOWN create with no id — sets nothing: no DELETE was sent, and what the read found enters `sweptOrders` for the next decision to name. Cleared by one thing only: a later cancel result that is `Absent`. A complete orders walk clears nothing in either direction: listing the order means the cancel did not take and the obligation stands; omitting it is not evidence (H-ORD-2a).
  - **`evaluateMarket`, wanted branch.** After the existing H-Q-5b overshoot branch and above the `!actionable` guard (its first leg is a cancel, I1): while the latch is set and `!cancelConfirmed[side]`, enqueue a `KindCancelConfirmPlace` with the side's role (`ReasonReduce` on a reducing side, `ReasonRequote` on an adding one), and never `decideSide` from a model that still carries the order. Never a standalone cancel: H-QUE-2 has no row for one on a reducing side, and on either side the replacement follows from the same verifying read that discharges the obligation (the `Absent` result drops the dependent placement and §6.5 re-derives it after the H-ORD-4b truth cycle). Cadence: every tick while the episode is younger than `position_poll_s`, then once per `position_poll_s`; `enqueue`'s per-side dedup keeps one sweep in flight.
  - **`evaluateMarket`, `!wanted` branch.** The existing re-sweep of a side being turned off (`atRisk > 0 || sweptOn`) also triggers on the latch, so an obligation is not carried silently into the next time the side is wanted.
  - **`build`, empty cancel.** The latch licenses the read-only sweep when the model holds nothing to name (`atRisk <= 0`). That read is exactly what discharges the latch; refusing it raised `WRITE_NOT_BUILDABLE` every tick and left the side undecided for ever (review finding 1).
  - **Absent branch, retirement by coid.** Besides the acked entries whose id the cancel named, the Absent branch now retires a pending entry whose coid a requested order carries — the UNKNOWN create a verifying read listed under its coid (`sweptOrders`) and a later cancel named from there. A complete read that listed the coid is H-ORD-2 clause 3's "found resting"; the sweep's complete read confirming the id absent is its "found cancelled". Both ends are positive identification, never absence. An entry whose coid no read ever listed is untouched (`TestConfirmedAbsenceDoesNotReleaseUnknownCreate` still holds).
  - **`CANCEL_UNVERIFIED` is raised once per episode** (on the latch's false→true transition) rather than once per sweep. With re-sweeps every 250 ms during a 1.5 s lag the old rule would have pushed about six SEV2s per requote. `SWEEP_PENDING` (SEV3) still journals every sweep and `SWEEP_INCOMPLETE` (SEV1) still pages once an order has stayed unconfirmed for `sweepPageBound` (5 s), as before. A sweep that named nothing still raises it as before.
  - **Turnover mirror.** `marketRuntime` carries the latch and its two stamps, and `saveMarket`/`loadMarket` copy them exactly as `cancelConfirmed`. `evaluateMarket`, `applyWriteResult` and `build` all run inside `marketContext`, so each market reads its own.
- **Behaviour that changed beyond the bug:**
  - An ADDING side with an unverified cancel is re-swept too. The same trap existed there (a requote inside the first poll interval left a phantom that suppressed the adding quote until the touch moved); the consequence was a presence gap rather than a missing exit.
  - A cancel the exchange silently failed to apply is now swept again until it is confirmed gone, on a wanted side as on a turned-off one.
  - An UNKNOWN create that a verifying read identified by coid and a later clean sweep confirmed cancelled is retired. Before, the id-less entry stood at |q| for the life of the process (pre-existing; reachable on HEAD whenever §6.5 kept wanting the move).
  - The SEV2 count per lag episode drops from one-per-sweep to one; the SEV3/SEV1 behaviour of the sweep itself is unchanged.
- **Unchanged:** H-FAIL-3 and H-ORD-4c semantics. Nothing is retired by absence or by elapsed time; `absentOrders`, `awaitCancelTruth` (H-ORD-4b) and the overshoot branch are as in candidate-6. `escalateUnresolved` still skips acked entries, so an acked-but-unlisted order that the re-sweep somehow cannot clear is reported by the sweep's own `SWEEP_INCOMPLETE` rather than by `ORDER_UNKNOWN`.

## Residual risk (not fixed here)

- **Write cost under a persistent lag.** While the exchange keeps listing a cancelled order, the wanted side spends one sweep per tick for the first `position_poll_s` (dedup keeps one in flight; each real sweep takes 0.5–1 s on Kalshi, so one or two per lag episode) and one per `position_poll_s` after that. The `!wanted` branch's own per-tick cadence for a side being turned off is unchanged from candidate-6.
- **Acked entries are still not escalated by §7.2.** If a re-sweep could never clear an entry, the operator hears about it through `SWEEP_INCOMPLETE`, not through an `ORDER_UNKNOWN`-style anomaly on the entry itself.
- **lip-8tl** (above): an order fully executed before its first walk, on a side that stays wanted at an unchanged touch, under-quotes that side until the touch moves. Pre-existing; the aggregate is over-counted, never under-counted.
